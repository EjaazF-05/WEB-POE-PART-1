Our Services

Cape Town Football Academy offers different training options to help players improve their football skills.

1 - on - 1 Training

Individual training allows players to work on their own skills with a coach.

Weekly Team Training and Matchdays

Players can take part in team training and matchdays. This helps players improve their teamwork and match skills.

Holiday Clinic

Holiday Clinics give young players the opportunity to train and improve their football skills during school holidays.
